# Checkpoint 879: bounded native Stop claim and error persistence

2026-10-14 UTC. RuntimeCommandExecutor now creates one fixed-database NativeStopDispatchWriter shared by every StopControlWorker produced by its factory. The worker offloads claimStopControl and recordStopControlError through the existing StateAccessFacade. This is a bounded migration slice, not a whole-runtime or deployment PASS.

Ordinary claims use Control1 with zero waiting queue. The immutable, bounded original is captured before verification awaits. Selected captures its authentic owner epoch token before ControlTurnVerifier.owned(), preserves that exact token, and maps stale claim-boundary custody to StoreIntegrityError. After verification, the writer mints the opaque same-owner lease, extracts its shared liveness proof, and submits synchronously without an intervening await. It retains the lease and target lock through actual native exit and receipt validation, including cancellation, timeout, capacity refusal and synchronous submission-return failure. Explicit and Mapped never read selection. Original generation, turn, mapping, job/hold custody and final RPC selection checks remain in place.

Any unconfirmed submitted claim is Unknown, including failed/rolled-back transactions, lost ACK, malformed/crossed ACK, source cancellation and deadline. One bounded sticky original is retained in the shared writer. Later polls and other factory-created workers throw that same unresolved result before reading another page; they do not wrap the cursor, fake an empty page, interrupt, fall back or retry it. The worker reports this degradation once. Confirmed null and proven pre-submission refusals do not set the barrier. Recovery processing is unchanged. This barrier is runtime-local fail-closed ownership, not cross-restart reconciliation.

A small bound ACK carries the claim token only. The parent reconstructs the immutable claim from its captured original and privately authenticates that successful receipt by object identity. Only that writer's one-use receipt can enter the bounded Control completion FIFO for error persistence. Clones, other writers and reuse are refused. Error writes require no Selected lease or fresh job/hold proof; they retain the original operation/token and dispatching-only UPDATE predicate, preserve non-dispatching phases, and limit diagnostics to 1000 Unicode scalars. Error-persistence uncertainty never redispatches or retries. Dispatch and persistence failures are retained together; exact dispatch cancellation skips the recorder.

Caller signals are isolated before native polling/Selected capture and inside the writer. Native source-state checks detect suppressed abort propagation and method replacement without executing caller hooks in slot setup/exit. Native SQLite is joined, never terminated to manufacture completion.

The claim transaction reuses the acceptance storage policy: original evidence/count limits before clone/JSON, transaction-local SQLite storage/type/byte/count preflights before current control, mapping, queue and hold decoding, cumulative repeated-read accounting across both validation passes, and bounded generated queue serialization. Trigger-expanded data is rechecked before the final decoders and rolls back atomically. Bigint identities above 2^53, REAL infinity timestamps, existing-only databases, original CAS and rollback semantics are preserved.

Repeated-read dispatch accounting is deliberately fail-closed and is not capacity parity with acceptance. A near-capacity original may be accepted successfully and later exceed the dispatch budget because control and hold evidence are charged again in both validation passes. Tests prove such a refusal rolls back the claim and establishes explicit unresolved native ownership instead of dropping or retrying the accepted original.
